Privacy Policy

App: ARROW ESCAPE  |  Developer: ByteWave Studio  |  Last updated: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ByteWave Studio ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the Android game ARROW ESCAPE ("the app").

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. We do not collect personal information ourselves.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (such as unlocked levels, stars, coins, hints, undos, and your sound, music and vibration settings) in a file on your own device. This data stays on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Advertising

The app may show ads, including rewarded ads that you can choose to watch to receive in-game rewards. Ads are provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. These services may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, to show and measure ads. They have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services

You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration: to give haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in the app's Settings.
  • Internet and network state: used to show ads.

5. Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help.

6. Data deletion

Because we do not store your personal data on our servers, you can delete all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact us
